How to read this

Group markets by their price. If Kalshi is calibrated, markets priced near 70¢ should resolve YES about 70% of the time, so each dot sits on the dashed diagonal. Bars show how much a dot could move by luck alone: with few markets they are wide.

Drag the sliders. Fewer buckets give tighter bars; a tail cutoff drops near-certain markets; a volume floor drops thin ones. If a flag appears and disappears as you slice, it was noise.
